## Full Description

ŞansLab is a fast, no-nonsense tool that generates random coupons for the Turkish chance games Sayısal Loto, Süper Loto, Şans Topu, On Numara and Hızlı On.

Pick a game, tap "Generate" — a fresh set of cryptographic-quality random numbers is ready. Generate a single row, or 10 rows at once. Don't like it? Tap "Regenerate".

FEATURES
• 5 games: Sayısal Loto (6+Joker), Süper Loto (6), Şans Topu (5+1), On Numara (10), Hızlı On (choose 1–10 numbers)
• Single coupon or batch of 10
• Generation modes: Pure Random, Balanced, Low-share, ŞansFormülü (Premium)
• ŞansFormülü: combinations scored via statistical analysis of the historical draw archive (does not improve odds)
• Modern "Night Draw" interface: glowing lottery balls, gold accents
• Turkish and English
• Light and dark theme
• Copy your generated coupon
• Works offline

PREMIUM (all games and single/10 generation are already FREE)
• Ad-free experience
• Balanced, Low-share and ŞansFormülü generation modes
• Number statistics panel (frequency + fairness read)
• Coupon history and favorites
• Unlimited, uninterrupted generation

AN HONEST NOTE
In games of chance every draw is independent. Past results do not predict future draws, and no method improves your odds of winning. ŞansLab is purely an entertainment tool that makes filling out a coupon easier — it makes no promise of winnings.

ŞansLab is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Milli Piyango, Sisal, or any game operator. No tickets are sold and no betting takes place inside the app.

Not for users under 18. Play responsibly.
